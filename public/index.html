How reliable is this data?

Official: METAR and TAF come from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center and are shown exactly as issued (raw text included). Many regional airports, including some in Ethiopia such as HAAM, publish no METAR or TAF. For those, this app never borrows another airport's report.

Model (◌, grey italic): Open-Meteo is a computer forecast on a grid several kilometres wide. It is not an observation, can differ from real conditions, and has no cloud ceiling. It is shown only when no official value exists for that time, and always labelled.

Nothing is invented: if a value is unavailable it says N/A. Always verify with your official AIS/MET service before flight.

Sources: NOAA Aviation Weather Center (METAR, TAF) · Open-Meteo (model values where a TAF has none). For training and situational awareness only. Not an official briefing. Always verify with your official AIS/MET service before flight.
